# Art Direction

## Visual Goal

Stylized, readable, toy-like battlefield presentation with a World War 1 theme. The game should feel charming and tactical without becoming visually noisy.

## Principles

- Readability beats realism.
- Units must be distinguishable at phone size.
- Terrain should communicate movement and defense effects clearly.
- Avoid copying the specific art, UI, characters, branding, or layout of existing games.
- Keep the mood adventurous and tactical rather than bleak.

## World War 1 Motifs

- Trenches
- Sandbags
- Mud
- Craters
- Field guns
- Early tanks
- Biplanes
- Barbed wire
- Command tents
- Signal flags

## Asset Tracking

Every external asset should eventually be recorded with:

- File name
- Source
- Creator
- License
- Commercial-use status
- Notes on modification
